# Insights are reflective only — "mirror, not doctor"

The roadmap is depth-led: the wedge is turning the clock dial from a record into an insight engine (patterns, predictions, "typical" bands, trends). Several candidate insights edge toward health interpretation — "your baby isn't having enough milk", "fewer dirty nappies, is everything ok?", "your baby should eat X". We draw a hard line: **the app reflects the baby's own data; it does not advise, reassure, diagnose, or prescribe.**

## Why

- **Liability and safety at audience C.** A public product that tells an anxious 2am parent "everything's fine" can be catastrophically wrong (a genuinely dehydrated baby), and "not enough milk" can trigger panic or overfeeding. Reassurance and alarm are both failure modes we can't stand behind for strangers.
- **Regulatory risk.** Interpreting health data and giving recommendations can pull a product toward medical/wellness-device classification. Staying reflective keeps us clearly a data-tracking tool.
- **Trust is the wedge's foundation.** "Your baby's data isn't the product; we just show it to you beautifully" is undermined the moment we start judging the parent's caregiving.

## The rule

- **Reflective, comparative-to-own-baseline:** "Today's total (420 ml) is below this baby's 7-day average (680 ml)." State the fact; the parent judges.
- **No diagnostic/reassuring language:** never "enough", "normal", "ok?", "should".
- **Health hand-off, not answer:** at most append "Big changes are worth mentioning to your pediatrician."
- **Suggestions only from fixed published guidance:** solids/weaning suggestions come from a published list (e.g. NHS/AAP stages) filtered by the baby's recorded likes/dislikes — never from an inference that *this* baby *needs* a given food.

## Consequences

- Insight copy is a reviewed surface, not ad-hoc strings — the phrasing *is* the safety boundary.
- Predictions ("next feed ~14:30") are framed as observations of the baby's own rhythm, not instructions.
- If we ever deliberately cross into advisory territory, it requires disclaimers and likely regulatory review before public launch — a conscious, separate decision, not a drift.
